Our Mission

Memories that feel real, honest, and beautifully alive.

At Love in Motion, we want your wedding memories to feel real — not posed or perfect, but genuine. We capture the laughter, the cries, the nerves and the beauty of it all, so you can look back and truly feel the day again.

We focus on behind-the-scenes moments that go unseen in traditional videography. Our timeless, share-ready reels make it easy to revisit and share your celebration instantly. By blending creativity with accessibility, we offer an affordable way to preserve your day beautifully, without losing any of its magic.

About Us

Meet Makenna

Hey, I’m Makenna — the heart behind Love in Motion. I’m a wedding content creator based in Southern California who’s obsessed with capturing the real, unposed moments that make your day yours.

I started Love in Motion because I noticed how fast wedding days fly by and how many sweet, in-between memories never make it to the camera roll.

At Love in Motion, my goal is simple, to deliver high-quality reels and intimate content that couples can relive and share with ease, without the overwhelming price tag.

I am here to give those intimate moments back — the giggles while getting ready, the nervous deep breaths before walking down the aisle, the way your partner looks at you when they think no one’s watching. Whether you’re running barefoot on the beach or dancing under string lights, I’m there to make sure your love stays in motion so you can relive it forever.

Every love story deserves to shine, and I’m honored to be the one holding the lens.
