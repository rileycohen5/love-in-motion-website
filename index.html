Our Mission

The Art of Remembering

At Love in Motion, we want your wedding memories to feel real - not posed or perfect, but genuine. Your wedding day is filled with moments you won't always get to see for yourself. The quiet interactions between loved ones, the smallest details, and everything happening in between the big moments.

Our purpose is to capture your day beyond what you experience firsthand, preserving the moments you remember and revealing the ones you never even knew happened.

Because sometimes, it's the moments you didn't see that mean the most.

By blending creativity with accessibility, we offer an affordable way to preserve your wedding day.

Our timeless, share-ready reels allow you to relive your favorite moments whenever you wish, right from your camera roll. Whether you're revisiting the memories yourself or sharing them with loved ones through a simple text or social media, your wedding day is always just a click away.

Behind the Lens

Meet Makenna

Hi there, I'm Makenna, the creative heart and eye behind Love in Motion.

My journey in the wedding industry began when I was just 14 years old, assisting a local wedding planner. From a young age, I had the opportunity to witness the beauty and all the emotions that go into creating such a meaningful celebration.

It was through those experiences that I began to realize some of the most beautiful moments are often the ones that go missed by traditional videography.

The quiet anticipation before walking down the aisle, the fleeting glances exchanged across a crowded room, the laughter shared when no one realizes they're being filmed. These are the moments that have always meant the most to me.

I don't believe in only documenting what your day looked like. I want you to be able to relive what it felt like.

I am honored to be welcomed into one of the most intimate and meaningful days of someone's life. My hope is that the memories I preserve become little pieces of your story that you find yourself returning to for years to come.
